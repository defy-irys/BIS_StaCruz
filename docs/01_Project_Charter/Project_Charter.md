# Project Overview

## Executive Summary
    1. Introduction
        This document explains the purpose of the development of this project, what problems it solves, who it's for, and how it'll be beneficial for the Local Government.

    2. Objectives
        Primarily; To develop a centralized, secure, digital system that reduces the use of paper for resident record management, profiling, and reports.

    3. Main Points
        The system is intended to enhance barangay operations by improving efficiency while perserving human oversight and decision-making.

    4. Potential Impact
        The development of this system will significantly reduce the workload on evaluators, make it easier for Barangay Officials to manage residents' records, and overall provide an easier time for those involved.

## Purpose
   To develop a centralized, secure, and scalable web-based Barangay Information Management System (BIMS) with an integrated Barangay Inhabitant Profiling System (BIPS) that streamlines resident record management, profiling, reporting, and future service integrations.

## Problem Statement
    Current Barangays lack a digitized system that assists in resident profiling, and records management conducted by the Local Government (Barangay) with problems such as fragmented records, inconsistent data, difficult analytics still being present with the use of manual reporting.

## Objectives
    The primary objectives of this project are to:
    - Digitalize and modernize barangay resident and household records
    - Simplify data collection and mainteinance
    - Automate report generation
    - Provide demographic and sectoral analytics
    - Integrate geographic information systems (GIS)
    - Establish a scalable architecture for future expansion

## Project Scope
    An Information Management System with an integrated Inhabitant Profiling System based on the DILG Records of Barangay Inhabitants (RBI) Framework. 
    
    Specifically designed to assist in resident profiling and records management by the Local Governments to ease with the use of paper and to simplify how records are created, and stored.

## Out of Scope
    As of current, The Barangay Information Management System - Barangay Inhabitant Profiling System (BIMS-BIPS) is intended to be designed around the specific needs of Local Governments for their Operations (or related use). 
    
    Version 1.0 is intended for deployment at the barangay level. 
    
    The project does not include deployment, customization, or operational support for agencies above the barangay level. Such Implementations may be undertaken as separate projects upon request and approval by the appropriate government authority.

## Success Criteria
    - Successfully manage resident and household records
    - Reduce reliance on paper-based processes
    - Generation of RBI-Compliant reports
    - Operate reliably in a production environment
    - Meet agreed functional requirements before deployment
